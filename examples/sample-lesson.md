# 2.2 Customer Discovery — Interviews Before Solutions

Before you build anything, talk to users. This lesson covers how to run discovery interviews and
how many you actually need. Submit your interview notes in the intake form below.

# Customer Discovery — Key Notes

**The core principle**
- Fall in love with the problem, not your solution. Interviews come *before* ideas.

**How to run an interview**
- 30–60 minutes; a pre-written question sequence, then follow "why?" on the fly.
- Ask about past behavior, not hypotheticals ("Tell me about the last time you…").
- Minimum **20 interviews** before you trust a pattern; incubators push for 100+.

**Then quantify**
- Surveys come *after* qualitative interviews — thousands of responses to validate, not discover.
- Surveys can't ask "why"; that's what interviews are for.

**Example**
- *Blue Apron* shadowed a busy parent, discovered the real value prop was pre-portioned ingredients,
  not recipes.

# customer_interview_template.pdf

CUSTOMER INTERVIEW TEMPLATE
1. Warm-up: Tell me about your role and a typical day.
2. Problem: Walk me through the last time you [did the task].
3. Current solution: What do you use today? What's frustrating about it?
4. Impact: How much time/money does this cost you?
5. Wrap: Who else should I talk to?
